Audience

Primary hypothesis

College students and young adults around 18–25; class, coffee shops, and casual outings.

Archetype

Ruler

Elegant, confident, and simple; poised without feeling formal.

Modernist

Clean structure, neutral colors, and simple typography. The annotated comparison remains project evidence.
