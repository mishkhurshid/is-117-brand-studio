01 / ORDER

Modernist

Functional hierarchy, a measured grid, and direct typography.

STAR Everyday / 01

A quieter kind of statement

Effortlessly Simple. Always Elegant.

A timeless everyday essential that brings simplicity and elegance to everyday outfits.

Explore the Collection

Reference

Herbert Bayer, Poster for Kandinsky exhibition honoring the artist's 60th birthday, 1926. Harvard Art Museums, object BR48.107.

Design choices
A strict two-column grid, a clear type scale, and short red rules translate the poster's forceful hierarchy and asymmetric markers into a functional storefront.
Intended audience effect
A direct route from the promise to the shirt should make the everyday styling idea quick to scan.
Risk
Too much order could feel stiff or formal instead of effortless.

02 / EXPRESSION

Postmodernist

Layering, contrast, and a more expressive typographic voice.

STAR Everyday / 01

A quieter kind of statement

Effortlessly Simple. Always Elegant.

A timeless everyday essential that brings simplicity and elegance to everyday outfits.

Explore the Collection

Reference

Paula Scher, The Diva is Dismissed, 1994. The Museum of Modern Art. MoMA describes the work's unorthodox spacing, mixed weights, and postmodern / New Wave approach.

Design choices
Offset color planes, a diagonal band, and a high-contrast serif headline borrow the reference's layered energy without copying its portrait or poster composition.
Intended audience effect
A more expressive frame may make a simple shirt feel distinctive and invite visitors to explore styling possibilities.
Risk
Layering could compete with the copy and weaken the brand's simple, confident voice.
