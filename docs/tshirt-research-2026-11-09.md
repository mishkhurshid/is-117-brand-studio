**Interpretation:** These examples show different emphases: UNIQLO foregrounds practical product guidance; Everlane offers detailed fit and material information; COS frames a basic tee through refined styling. This is a comparison of site communication, not a claim about sales or audience preference.

## Two positions to consider

1. **Practical, value-led everyday basics.** Lead with usefulness, straightforward sizing, and casual outfits. Risk: STAR may become hard to distinguish from large basics retailers; a low-price promise would be unverified.
2. **Effortless minimalist elegance (chosen working direction).** Lead with versatile, simple styling and a clean visual identity, while keeping functional product information accessible. Risk: style alone will not answer fit or material questions.

**Decision:** Keep option 2 for now. It fits STAR's existing modernist design, Ruler brand character, and elegant/confident/simple voice. The choice is a design hypothesis, not a validated customer preference.

## Two short persona hypotheses

- **Primary — student everyday dresser:** College student or young adult, about 18–25, choosing an outfit for class, coffee, or casual outings. Wants a simple tee that looks put together without much effort. May hesitate without fit guidance. **Assumption:** this audience values styling versatility and clean design.
- **Secondary — young professional:** Young professional, about 23–30, choosing a tee for casual workwear or a polished weekend outfit. Wants something easy to pair with trousers or a blazer. May need clearer product measurements before deciding. **Assumption:** this audience values a slightly more dressed-up presentation.

**Chosen audience:** the primary student/young-adult audience. The second persona is a useful contrast, not a claim that either group has been interviewed.

## Look, brand character, and honest persuasion

- **Look:** Modernist layout, restrained neutral colors, readable typography, and the simple STAR wordmark; avoid clutter.
- **Character:** Ruler archetype, expressed as elegant, confident, and simple rather than exclusive or intimidating.
- **Honest persuasion:** The free three-outfit styling guide offers value without requiring purchase (reciprocity). Optional Relaxed / Layered / Polished style choices give relevant suggestions without changing the demo bag (voluntary consistency). Do not invent scarcity, reviews, fabric claims, sustainability claims, or product measurements.
